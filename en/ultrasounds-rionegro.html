Ultrasound studies you can ask about

How it works: LABCCO coordinates appointments with IMEDI, our diagnostic-imaging partner at C.C. Rionegro Plaza, second floor, a short distance from our laboratory. Prices are not published here; contact us to confirm the current study and quote.

Abdomen and urinary tract

Total abdominal, upper-abdominal and urinary-tract ultrasound including kidneys, bladder and transabdominal prostate, according to the order.

Gynecological

Transvaginal and transabdominal pelvic gynecological ultrasound.

Obstetric

Transvaginal and transabdominal obstetric ultrasound according to the treating professional's request.

Other modalities

Thyroid, breast, soft-tissue, testicular, joint, Doppler and other IMEDI options are confirmed by order and availability.

Preparation and results

Preparation changes by study: some require a full bladder, fasting or other instructions. Send us the exact study name so we can confirm the correct preparation.

Results are sent the same day or at most the next business day, depending on the study and booking confirmation.

Need to schedule or confirm a study?

Imaging appointments are requested through LABCCO. Send us the study name or order so we can confirm availability, preparation and delivery.

Radiographs available according to the order

How it works: LABCCO coordinates appointments with IMEDI, our diagnostic-imaging partner at C.C. Rionegro Plaza, second floor, a short distance from our laboratory. Prices are not published here; contact us to confirm the current study and quote.

Extremities and joints

Shoulder, elbow, arm, forearm, wrist, hand, fingers, hip, knee, leg, ankle, foot and calcaneus.

Spine, chest and abdomen

Cervical, thoracic and lumbosacral spine; chest and plain abdominal radiographs.

Skull and face

Skull, nasal bones, sinuses, orbits, mastoids, face and other projections.

Special projections

Comparative and additional views (oblique, tunnel or stress) when indicated.

Before you attend

Send us the order or exact radiograph name. We will confirm whether an appointment is needed, the body region and projections, and any special instructions.

Results are sent the same day or at most the next business day, depending on the coordinated study.

Need to schedule or confirm a study?

Imaging appointments are requested through LABCCO. Send us the study name or order so we can confirm availability, preparation and delivery.

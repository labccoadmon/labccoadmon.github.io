DNA paternity testing with Universidad de Antioquia

LABCCO coordinates one DNA paternity test through an agreement with Universidad de Antioquia. The sample is collected by a finger prick to obtain a drop of blood, and the service is available for participants of all ages.

Delivery time: up to 10 business days from the time the sample reaches Universidad de Antioquia. Confirm the case requirements before booking.

What to expect

  1. Contact LABCCO and describe your case.
  2. We coordinate the appointment and the documents required for the participants.
  3. The sample is collected at LABCCO.
  4. The university processes the test and the result is coordinated by the laboratory.

The test uses approximately 12 genetic markers as a general reference; the percentage and interpretation depend on the case and report.

International and legal use

Ask us about the requirements for your specific legal or international procedure before booking. Requirements can vary by authority and case.

Payment is accepted in Colombian pesos in cash or by Bancolombia transfer. Results are coordinated by email.

Glucose testing

LABCCO performs blood glucose testing for individual patients and occupational health requests. We can also review requests for other diabetes-related laboratory tests and confirm what is available for your order.

Preparation depends on the requested test. Contact us before attending so we can confirm whether your order requires a specific preparation.

For patients and companies

Individual testing

Bring your identification document and medical order, if you have one.

Occupational health

For companies, we quote the requested tests according to the worker, role and evaluation.

Results are sent by email. Ask us for the delivery time of the specific test.

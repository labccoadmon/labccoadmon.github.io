UNDERSTAND YOUR REFERRAL

Which examination do you need?

Gynecological and obstetric scans have different purposes. If you are pregnant, share your referral so the requested examination can be arranged.

Transvaginal ultrasound

A vaginal probe produces pelvic images. The professional explains the examination before performing it.

Transabdominal ultrasound

The examination is performed over the skin of the lower abdomen.

Pelvic ultrasound

If the referral does not specify the approach, send it to us so the examination can be clarified.

BEFORE YOUR VISIT

Preparing for your examination

Bladder preparation differs by approach. Confirm your instructions and tell the professional if pregnancy is possible or if you have concerns about the examination.

Have these ready

  • Your identification document.
  • Your referral, if you have one, with the full examination name.
  • The requested area, side and scan type.
  • The instructions confirmed when you book.

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose the ultrasound approach?

Discuss your concerns and preferences before the appointment. The appropriate approach should be agreed with the professional based on the referral.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Imaging examinations need advance coordination. Contact LABCCO to check availability and preparation.

How much does the examination cost?

Request a quote on WhatsApp. The area, side, views or contrast protocol may change the scope. We confirm the quote for the examination you need.

Where does the scan take place?

It takes place at our specialist partner centre in Rionegro. LABCCO coordinates the booking and confirms the location before you attend.

When will I receive the results?

Results are sent the same day or by the next business day, depending on the examination and booking confirmation.

Are Sunday appointments available?

We do not offer services on Sundays. Ask about available weekday and Saturday appointments.

Is care provided in English?

This page provides information in English. English-speaking clinical staff are not guaranteed. Please discuss any language support needs with LABCCO before booking.
